Does membership guarantee a remote job?

No. Membership provides learning, career guidance, community and opportunity updates. Hiring decisions are made by employers and depend on role requirements, applicant capability and the employer's process.

Is this suitable if I am not looking for remote work?

Yes. The career mapping and professional development elements can also support career clarity, role transitions, workplace growth and professional positioning beyond remote roles.

Does the subscription include every SkeelUP Africa bootcamp?

Membership provides access to selected bootcamps and training opportunities as announced. Any programme with a separate fee or additional eligibility requirement will be identified before registration.
