YIELD Global Impact · Mentorship & Career Development

Be the mentor you wish you had.

MentorshipMatters Bootcamp is a guidance, empowerment and career-development initiative created to unlock potential in children, teenagers and young adults, especially those with limited access to informed guidance, role models and opportunity pathways.

Through career mapping, clarity clinics and access to mentors across different fields of life interests, the initiative helps participants move from questions and uncertainty toward clearer direction and practical next steps.

The Bootcamp Model

Clarity for the journey. Guidance for the next step.

MentorshipMatters creates structured spaces where young people can explore their interests, understand possible pathways, ask better questions and learn from people with relevant experience.

01

Career Mapping

Help participants identify interests, strengths, capabilities and possible fields of study, work or enterprise. Turn discovery into a practical personal development and career direction map.

  • Interests and strengths discovery
  • Career and pathway exploration
  • Personal action map and next steps
02

Clarity Clinics

Focused guidance sessions that help participants bring specific questions, explore choices and get practical direction on education, skills, career decisions and personal development.

  • Career and education questions
  • Skills and opportunity direction
  • Decision-making and goal clarity
03

Mentorship Access

Connect participants with mentors, professionals and practitioners whose experience can help them understand a field, build confidence and take informed next steps.

  • Mentor conversations and role modelling
  • Guidance across different fields of interest
  • Learning, exposure and accountability
From Potential to Pathway

A practical journey through the bootcamp.

Each cohort or activation can be organised around a simple sequence that takes participants from self-discovery to direction, relationships and action.

01 · DiscoverExplore interests, strengths, aspirations and questions.
02 · MapDevelop a clearer career or personal development pathway with practical next steps.
03 · ConnectEngage mentors, experienced practitioners and peer learning opportunities.
04 · MoveTranslate guidance into learning actions, goals and follow-through.
A Mentorship Movement

There is a place for mentors, partners and institutions.

For mentees

Children, teenagers and young adults seeking direction, encouragement, guidance and exposure to different fields of life interest.

For mentors

Professionals, entrepreneurs, skilled practitioners, creatives, educators and leaders willing to share experience, provide perspective and guide emerging talent.

For schools & partners

Schools, community organisations, youth groups, employers, donors and institutions interested in widening access to mentorship and career guidance.

Be the mentor you wish you had.

The initiative invites people to turn their experience into a bridge for someone else's progress. Mentors and partners can contribute through guided conversations, skills exposure, career information, learning opportunities and support for bootcamp delivery.

Responsible Mentorship

A safe, respectful and age-appropriate experience.

Mentorship involving children and young people must be designed with appropriate safeguarding, consent and accountability. Programme delivery will use age-appropriate participation arrangements, mentor orientation and clear boundaries. For participants under 18, parents or guardians and relevant partner institutions will be involved in the required consent and safeguarding process.

Cohort dates, participation requirements, mentor matching and delivery details will be communicated when each bootcamp cycle is announced.
