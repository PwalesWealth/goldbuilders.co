# HR & Leadership Solution — HR Outsourcing Suite Update

## Added to the GoldBuilders Services page
The HR & Leadership solution now includes an HR Outsourcing Suite section with three service lines:

1. **Talent Recruitment & Selection** — role profiling, job description support, candidate sourcing and screening, interview coordination, shortlist preparation, reference checks, and offer/onboarding coordination.
2. **Background Checks** — role-relevant, consent-based screening to validate material candidate information, including identity, credentials, education, employment history, references and relevant licences, subject to lawful access and authorisation.
3. **Staff Verification** — verification of existing workforce records, identity, qualifications, certifications, licences, employment history and references, with an exceptions register and documented follow-up.

The services can be engaged individually or scoped as a managed HR outsourcing arrangement. No new pricing was invented; recruitment and verification services are marked for a custom quote based on workforce size, role complexity, hiring volume, check types, lawful record access and turnaround requirements.

## Added to Nexora Solutions
The **HR & Leadership** solution catalogue now includes quote-based options for:
- HR Outsourcing Suite
- Talent Recruitment & Selection
- Background Checks & Candidate Screening
- Staff Verification & Workforce Records Review

Existing people and leadership offers, published indicative fees, consultation workflow, selection interface and WhatsApp enquiry flow are retained.

## Safeguards
Background checks and staff verification are framed as role-related, proportionate and confidential services. They require appropriate notice and consent or another lawful basis, lawful access to records, fair handling of discrepancies, and an opportunity for clarification where appropriate. Unverified information is not treated as an automatic rejection basis.
