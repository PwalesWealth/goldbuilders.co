## Pricing and scope notes
All amounts are proposed indicative GoldBuilders professional starting fees, not guaranteed fixed quotations. Final fees depend on headcount, volume, jurisdiction, data quality, integrations, delivery scope and timeline. Government and regulator charges, statutory taxes, stamp duties, external counsel, third-party licences, cloud/API usage, hosting, hardware, ad spend and vendor fees are excluded unless an approved written proposal expressly includes them.

Tax opinions, regulated tax representation, legal opinions, and statutory external audit opinions are delivered by or coordinated with appropriately qualified independent professionals where required. The internal audit offer is an internal controls and process review and is not represented as a statutory external audit opinion. Incorporation and filing services are coordination and documentation support, with official fees and any regulated professional work separately identified.
