LuxeStreams Service Menu

You do not have to join the Concierge Club to use LuxeStreams. Individuals, families and businesses can request a single service or a recurring arrangement. The Club is an optional relationship and service-coordination pathway; charges apply only to services you approve. Choose one service or combine several services in the basket below. Starting rates are confirmed against location, scope, timing and provider availability before payment.

Errands & Personal Assistance

₦10,000 from / standard task

One standard errand within one local area, up to 60 minutes of coordination.

₦18,000 from / executive errand

Business or multi-stop errand, up to two hours. Additional active time: ₦4,000/hour.

Urgent / same-day priority: add 30% (minimum ₦5,000). Shopping budget and transport are separate.

Pickup, Delivery & Dispatch

₦3,500 coordination fee per dispatch

Includes request handling, dispatch coordination and delivery follow-up. Courier/rider fare is charged separately at the confirmed route rate.

Multi-stop, bulky, fragile, high-value, out-of-hours and intercity deliveries are quoted before dispatch.

Rider fares depend on route, distance, timing and availability. The confirmed fare is shared before dispatch.

Shopping & Procurement

10% of purchase value

Buy-for-me, market runs and product sourcing. Minimum service fee ₦5,000. Goods budget, delivery and special sourcing costs are separate.

A purchase budget is agreed before the team spends on the client's behalf. Receipts are provided where available.

Standard Home Cleaning

₦25,000–₦30,000 1-bedroom

2-bedroom: ₦35,000–₦45,000 · 3-bedroom: ₦40,000–₦55,000 · 4-bedroom: ₦45,000–₦65,000 · duplex: ₦55,000–₦80,000.

Final amount depends on property condition, location, size, access and cleaning supplies.

Deep & Special Cleaning

₦30,000–₦40,000 1-bedroom deep clean

2-bedroom: ₦45,000–₦60,000 · 3-bedroom: ₦55,000–₦75,000 · duplex: ₦80,000–₦120,000. Post-construction, move-in/out and after-event cleaning are quoted after a scope check.

Special chemicals, waste removal, equipment hire and unusually soiled spaces may attract additional charges.

Office & Recurring Logistics

₦85,000 from / site visit or service day

Small-office cleaning and recurring workplace support are quoted after a location and task review. Laundry pickup/drop-off coordination: ₦5,000 from, plus provider charges.

Multi-site, scheduled, volume and corporate arrangements receive a written service plan and quote.

Flight Booking Assistance

₦5,000 domestic / ticket

International ticketing assistance: ₦10,000 per ticket. Fare, airline charges, baggage and change fees are separate and subject to supplier rules.

Hotels & Travel Planning

5% of hotel booking value

Minimum booking coordination fee ₦10,000. Itinerary design: ₦25,000 domestic; ₦50,000 international or multi-city. Hotel and travel costs are separate.

Visa & Airport Support

₦40,000 from / visa admin support

Document organisation and appointment-process assistance only. Embassy, visa-centre and third-party fees are separate; no visa outcome is guaranteed. Airport transfer arrangement: provider cost plus 10% coordination (₦5,000 minimum).

Personal Concierge Retainer

₦75,000 from / month

Recurring personal coordination for up to four standard requests per month, such as errands, bookings, pickups or lifestyle support. Scope, response times and rollover rules are agreed in writing.

Third-party purchases, courier fares, travel, provider and other pass-through charges are separate.

Corporate Concierge Retainer

₦250,000 from / month

Recurring business support for office logistics, executive requests, business travel coordination and scheduled service needs. The monthly fee is scoped to request volume, locations and service hours.

A service-level agreement, request cap, response times and supplier charges are confirmed before commencement.

Credit Consultation & Financing Support

1% · 2% · 1% consultation · processing · disbursement

Discuss salary advance, personal credit or business financing options, prepare your application and get help with documents, eligibility and disbursement follow-up with a lending partner.

Initial credit enquiryPathway discussion₦0
Consultation & application preparationOn requested credit amount · min ₦5,0001%
Processing / managementOn approved credit amount · min ₦5,0002%
Disbursement coordinationOn amount disbursed · min ₦2,5001%

Example: on a ₦500,000 facility, estimated LuxeStreams fees are ₦5,000 + ₦10,000 + ₦5,000 = ₦20,000. Interest, repayment terms and any lender-set charges are separate and are stated in the lender's written offer. Fees are confirmed in writing before you accept anything. Credit approval is decided by the lending institution, and no one can guarantee approval or release an approved loan for a fee.
