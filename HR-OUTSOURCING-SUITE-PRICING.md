These are proposed GoldBuilders starting rates added to the website for commercial planning. They are not represented as rates previously approved by management. Confirm delivery capacity, provider costs and tax treatment before treating them as binding quotations.

## Scope boundaries

- The ₦350,000 monthly Core HR Outsourcing Desk is a starting retainer for one organisation and up to 20 employees. It covers agreed HR administration, employee record upkeep, onboarding/offboarding coordination, HR calendar/policy documentation support, people operations check-ins and a monthly summary. Recruitment placement fees and external verification/provider charges are separate. Larger headcounts, multiple entities or high-touch employee relations support are quoted.
- Recruitment is charged only for a successful placement at 10% of the successful candidate's agreed annual gross salary, with a minimum professional fee of ₦200,000 per role. Advertising, assessment tools, travel and external checks are not included unless stated in a written quote.
- Background check tiers are indicative. Exact checks depend on role relevance, authorised record access, candidate notice and consent or another lawful basis, available provider coverage and timing. Statutory, specialist and third-party search charges are disclosed before work starts. No criminal, sanctions, credit or other sensitive search is implied unless it is specifically lawful, available and expressly included.
- Core staff verification (₦15,000) covers a focused identity/personnel-record and agreed employment-detail review. Enhanced staff verification (₦35,000) adds agreed qualification/professional credential, employment-history and reference checks where authorised. Bulk exercises are custom-quoted.
- VAT where applicable and third-party provider charges are excluded unless an approved proposal expressly includes them.
- The website labels fees as indicative starting prices; the written scope and proposal govern each engagement.
