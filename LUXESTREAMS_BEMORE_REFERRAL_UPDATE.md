# LuxeStreams, BeMore and Referral Partner Update

## Website updates
- Added a visible LuxeStreams price menu for one-off/non-member and corporate customers. The menu covers errands, dispatch, procurement, home and deep cleaning, office support, travel booking coordination, hotel planning, visa administration and airport transfers.
- Added an explicit explanation on the Club page that one-off services are available without joining the Club.
- Added BeMore pricing for one-to-one and executive coaching, group programmes, the two monthly memberships, ARIS 10X, workshops, retreats and workforce programmes.
- Added a public Referral Partner registration page. Once a partner registers, the page provides 47 offer-specific tracked referral destinations across GoldBuilders Co., LuxeStreams, BeMore Coaching, Kohen ventures and YIELD Global Impact. LuxeStreams logistics/concierge services and BeMore coaching, membership, ARIS 10X and corporate offers each have their own shareable destination links.
- Added referral CTA to public page footers and a 30-day first/last stored referral-code cookie mechanism.

## Backend and reporting
- Referral codes are generated by the server and partners start in Pending Review status.
- `/r/:code` routes only to allowlisted internal pages, logs clicks and sets the referral code on the destination URL.
- Supported lead endpoints attach the validated code to their SQLite records.
- Admin dashboard includes partner status, link clicks, attributed lead counts and a protected manual Referral Sales Register. Authorised staff can record closed sales by partner code, business unit, offer, currency, gross payment and eligible net service revenue, then mark the record verified, paid or reversed.
- The update adds forward-only SQLite migrations and referral partner/click tables. Preserve production `.env` and database files.

## Commercial approval caveats
- LuxeStreams and BeMore rates are proposed Nigerian public starting rates, not previously approved tariffs. Management should approve them before live publication.
- Third-party courier, cleaning, travel, lodging, vendor, official and purchase costs are displayed separately or subject to a confirmed quote.
- Partner commission rates, payment triggers, refunds, clawbacks, taxes and payout timing are not yet activated. Sign and approve a Referral Partner Agreement before promising a commission.
- Referral click and lead attribution are captured automatically. Closed sales are entered manually by an authorised team member in the Referral Sales Register and then finance-verified. The platform does not automatically reconcile bank receipts or invoices. A commission amount is recorded only when an approved rate is entered after the signed partner agreement and management approval.

## Installation
1. Back up the current production files and SQLite database.
2. Upload/merge the included updated files without replacing `.env`, the production database, admin login data or unrelated custom files.
3. Ensure the Node.js app runs the updated `server.js` and `public` folder.
4. Restart the application so SQLite migrations and referral tables are created.
5. Test partner registration, generate a link, follow it in a private browser window, submit a test LuxeStreams or BeMore enquiry, and verify referral code/click/lead in the protected dashboard.
6. Deploy the website on the canonical HTTPS domain before inviting partners. Referral links are generated from the domain on which the partner signup page is opened (`window.location.origin`); `APP_BASE_URL` is used elsewhere for server-generated links such as assessment results, not for these referral links.
