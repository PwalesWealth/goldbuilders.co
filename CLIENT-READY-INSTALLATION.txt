GOLDBUILDERS CLIENT-READY REFERRAL & CHECKOUT UPDATE

PATCH INSTALLATION
1. Back up the live website/application folder and production SQLite database.
2. Extract the ZIP into the application root (the folder containing server.js), allowing files to overwrite their matching paths.
3. Preserve the existing .env file, database files, private uploads and hosting credentials. Do not replace them with sample files.
4. Restart the Node.js application. server.js will add the new partner_type column to an existing referral_partners table when it starts.
5. Test:
   - /referral-partner.html
   - /referral-partner/
   - /become-a-referral-partner/
   - /subsidiaries/luxestreams.html
   - /subsidiaries/bemore.html
   - /admin/dashboard.html (must redirect to login when not authenticated)

WHAT CHANGED
- Referral registration now supports Affiliate Marketer, Reseller and Independent Sales Representative roles.
- Each partner receives a unique code and 49 offer-specific tracked links.
- The partner type is recorded in the protected admin referral register.
- LuxeStreams and BeMore price cards are selectable and support a combined multi-service checkout request.
- Referral buttons are placed immediately after the price cards, not in the footer.
- Proof of Work notes and client-facing backend implementation language have been removed or reworded.
- LuxeStreams credit card shows no-charge initial pathway discussion and lender-disclosed processing / disbursement charges; no arbitrary LuxeStreams percentage has been invented.

CHECKOUT NOTE
The new basket submits an order/enquiry request; it does not take card or bank payment automatically. Your team confirms availability and variable fees, then sends the approved payment instruction. A payment gateway integration is separate.

REFERRAL COMMISSION NOTE
Partner links track referrals. Commission rates and payment require an approved Referral Partner Agreement and manual sale verification. Credit / loan referral commission remains disabled unless required regulatory approval and written partner terms are in place.

This patch was prepared and locally validated. It has not been deployed to your live hosting account.
