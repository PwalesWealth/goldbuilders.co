People Operations · HR Outsourcing Suite

Recruit well. Verify responsibly. Build a workforce you can trust.

GoldBuilders Co. supports organisations that need reliable recruitment and workforce verification processes without having to build every HR capability in-house. Services can be engaged individually or combined into a managed HR outsourcing arrangement.

01 / Talent

Talent Recruitment & Selection

Structured support to define roles, attract suitable candidates and coordinate a fair, evidence-based selection process.

10% of annual gross salary
Minimum professional fee: ₦200,000 per successful hire.

  • Role profiling and job description support
  • Candidate sourcing and application screening
  • Interview coordination and shortlist preparation
  • Reference checks and offer / onboarding coordination
02 / Screening

Background Checks

Role-relevant, consent-based screening to help employers validate information provided during recruitment and make informed hiring decisions.

₦25,000 / ₦55,000 / ₦95,000
Essential, Standard and Comprehensive · per candidate.

  • Identity and credential checks
  • Education and employment history checks
  • Professional references and relevant licences
  • Additional checks where lawful, relevant and appropriately authorised
03 / Workforce

Staff Verification

Verification support for existing employees and workforce records, helping organisations confirm key employment and qualification details.

From ₦15,000 per employee
Enhanced verification: ₦35,000 per employee.

  • Staff identity and personnel record validation
  • Qualification, certification and licence verification
  • Employment history and reference validation
  • Exception reporting and documented follow-up

One suite. Flexible engagement.

Core HR outsourcing starts from ₦350,000 per month for up to 20 employees, subject to the agreed service scope. Recruitment placement fees and external verification/provider charges are priced separately.

Choose a single recruitment or verification service, or discuss an ongoing HR outsourcing arrangement based on your headcount, hiring volume, role requirements and internal HR capacity.

Safeguards: checks are scoped to legitimate role-related purposes and carried out with appropriate notice, consent or other lawful basis, access controls and confidentiality. Any adverse or inconsistent result should be validated and handled fairly, with an opportunity for the individual to clarify where appropriate. Service availability depends on applicable law and access to authorised records.
