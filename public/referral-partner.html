Questions

Good to know.

What counts as a referral?

An identifiable new prospect who enters through your link and submits a supported enquiry form. A sale becomes commission-eligible only after the company verifies the source, confirms the order and receives cleared payment under the approved agreement.

How long does my link keep credit?

Up to 30 days after a visitor clicks your link, subject to their browser settings.

Do I need approval before I share links?

You can share links straight away for attribution. Your status starts as Pending Review. Commission eligibility needs approval and a signed referral agreement.
