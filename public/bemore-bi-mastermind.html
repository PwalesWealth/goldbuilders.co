A practical monthly rhythm

Frame. Analyse. Explain. Apply.

Each month connects business questions to practical analysis and clear recommendations.

Week 1 · Frame

Define the business question, the decision to support and the measures that matter.

Week 2 · Analyse

Practise data organisation, quality checks, calculations and exploratory analysis.

Week 3 · Visualise

Develop a clear chart, report or dashboard view that makes the pattern easier to understand.

Week 4 · Communicate

Summarise the insight, explain its limits and recommend a practical next step.

Do I need to be an experienced data analyst?

No. The community can support beginners and working professionals. You should be willing to learn, practise and bring questions from real work or business contexts.

Does membership include software licences?

No software licence is promised as part of the monthly membership. Members should use available tools or free versions where appropriate; any additional software requirement will be communicated separately.

Is this one-to-one consulting for my company?

The subscription is a group mastermind and learning experience. Bespoke business intelligence consulting, dashboard development or company-specific analysis can be discussed and scoped separately.
